# First community experiment

## Name

The First Believers Field

## Duration

7 days.

## Objective

Test whether people will participate in a shared trust artifact before being asked to pay for the DEX.

## Day-by-day loop

### Day 1 — Identity

Publish the token identity card, creator context, and the ten-item preflight.

### Day 2 — Emotion

Publish the values/emotions statement and one image from the collection. Ask: “Which emotion made you stop?”

### Day 3 — Mechanism

Publish the bot transparency card, including one known failure or false signal.

### Day 4 — Collection

Show the Print.World constitution draft. Ask the community to identify one missing rule.

### Day 5 — Receipt

Run one public check: holder distribution, lock, burn, migration state, or funding path. Publish the source and limitations.

### Day 6 — Mission

Invite one non-financial contribution: a correction, artifact, source, falsifier, or visual trait.

### Day 7 — Decision

Publish the scoreboard:

- substantive questions;
- receipts submitted;
- corrections made;
- missions completed;
- collection interest;
- unresolved claims;
- evidence for or against paying the DEX.

## Success condition

At least 10 substantive responses or 3 independently submitted receipts within seven days, with participation from people who are not already current token holders.

## Failure condition

The response is only price talk, slogans, requests for calls, or pressure to pay. If that happens, the trust-field proposition has not yet earned expansion.

## What this experiment does not measure

- token price;
- trading volume;
- financial return;
- NFT resale value;
- DEX success;
- the creator's personal popularity.

Those are different variables. The market has enough ways to confuse them.
