# GTM loop

This repository adapts the GTM structure already developed for the Memewitz work.

## 1. Market / problem

Meme markets compress attention faster than they create trust. Bots can create activity, DEX payment can become a terminal signal, and community funding can make the first shared act a transaction rather than a relationship.

## 2. Audience

- first-believer collectors;
- bot-curious meme-market participants;
- creators who want a public trust preflight;
- community moderators and researchers;
- readers who want a mechanism before a market call.

The audience unit is a question, receipt, correction, or completed mission—not a wallet address.

## 3. Values and emotional entry

Lead with the emotional field in `docs/VALUES_AND_EMOTIONS.md`:

- suspicious enough to inspect;
- tender enough to include non-buyers;
- playful enough to remember;
- candid enough to show failure;
- patient enough to delay the DEX.

## 4. Proof / artifacts

- ten-item checklist;
- 101-item field manual;
- Print.World first-believer collection;
- claim ledger;
- bot transparency cards;
- migration readiness cards;
- failure log;
- weekly community scorecard;
- 101-image visual collection.

## 5. Offer

The first offer is not a token call. It is a way to participate in the trust field:

1. read the preflight;
2. submit a claim or falsifier;
3. complete a community mission;
4. earn or mint an identity artifact under published rules;
5. return with a correction or receipt.

## 6. Distribution

Use the existing Memewitz/Marvin surface:

- short mechanism posts;
- image cards from the collection;
- the book as a serialized thread or page release;
- weekly receipt board;
- community questions;
- creator and collector collaborations;
- GitHub as the canonical public evidence shelf.

## 7. Conversion

Primary conversion: a substantive response containing a question, source, receipt, or falsifier.

Secondary conversion: a completed mission or voluntary collection participation.

Do not use purchases, volume, likes, or impressions as trust metrics.

## 8. Falsifiers

Revise the GTM if:

- people want token calls but ignore the artifacts;
- the only successful CTA is community payment;
- the collection has no meaning independent of the token;
- claims repeatedly outrun receipts;
- the bot story creates more confusion than understanding;
- no participants appear outside current holders.

## 9. Operating loop

`Observe → Separate fact from interpretation → Publish receipt → Invite correction → Run mission → Update collection/ledger → Reassess DEX decision`

The loop must be able to stop. A trust field that cannot say “not yet” is only a funnel wearing a lab coat.
